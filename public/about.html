Rental Rules & Requirements (Govt of India Compliance)

1. Driving License & Age

Riders must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid Indian Driving License (or International Driving Permit for foreign nationals).

2. Mandatory Helmet Law

As per Section 129 of the Motor Vehicles Act, helmets are mandatory for both rider and pillion. We provide 2 ISI-approved helmets free of charge.

3. Fuel Policy

All bikes are handed over with sufficient petrol to reach the nearest fuel station. Electric EVs are provided with at least 80% charge.
